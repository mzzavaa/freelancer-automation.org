You write the same emails over and over. Project kickoff emails. Follow-up emails. "Thanks for your payment" emails. Invoice reminders. Each one slightly different, but mostly the same.

Templates help, but they feel robotic. Fully custom emails are personal but slow.

AI gives you the best of both: templates that adapt to each situation automatically.

## The Problem with Traditional Templates

Standard templates have gaps you fill in:

> "Hi [NAME], Thank you for your interest in [SERVICE]. I'd love to discuss your [PROJECT TYPE] needs..."

This works, but:
- You still spend time customizing
- The result often feels templated
- You miss opportunities to reference specific details from their inquiry

## How AI-Powered Templates Work

Instead of fill-in-the-blank templates, you give AI:
1. The incoming email (context)
2. Your template library (structure)
3. Your voice guidelines (tone)

AI generates a response that uses the right template structure but incorporates specific details from the conversation.

## Building Your Template Library

### Essential Freelancer Templates

**1. Initial Inquiry Response**
```
Purpose: Reply to potential client's first contact
Include: Acknowledgment, relevant experience mention, next step (call/meeting)
Tone: Warm, professional, confident
```

**2. Project Proposal Follow-up**
```
Purpose: Check in after sending a proposal
Include: Reference to proposal, offer to answer questions, soft deadline
Tone: Helpful, not pushy
```

**3. Project Kickoff**
```
Purpose: Start a new project officially
Include: Excitement, timeline recap, immediate next steps, communication expectations
Tone: Energetic, organized
```

**4. Progress Update**
```
Purpose: Keep client informed mid-project
Include: What's done, what's next, any blockers, timeline status
Tone: Clear, professional
```

**5. Deliverable Handoff**
```
Purpose: Send completed work
Include: What's attached, how to review, feedback request, next steps
Tone: Proud but not boastful
```

**6. Invoice Sent**
```
Purpose: Accompany an invoice
Include: Invoice reference, amount, due date, payment methods, thank you
Tone: Professional, appreciative
```

**7. Payment Reminder**
```
Purpose: Follow up on overdue invoice
Include: Invoice reference, original due date, request for update
Tone: Firm but polite
```

**8. Project Wrap-up**
```
Purpose: Close out a completed project
Include: Summary of work, final deliverables, testimonial request, future availability
Tone: Grateful, forward-looking
```

## The AI Prompt System

### Step 1: Create Your Master Prompt

Save this as your email assistant prompt:

```
You are my email assistant. I'll give you:
1. An incoming email or situation
2. Which template type to use
3. Any specific points to include

Generate a response that:
- Matches my voice: [professional but warm / casual and direct / etc.]
- References specific details from their message
- Follows the template structure but feels personal
- Is concise (under 150 words unless complexity requires more)
- Ends with a clear next step or call to action

My templates:
[paste your template descriptions]
```

### Step 2: Use It

When you need to respond:

```
Incoming email:
[paste the email you received]

Template: Initial Inquiry Response

Additional context: They mentioned they found me through [referral source]. Their timeline seems urgent.
```

### Step 3: Review and Send

AI generates a draft. You:
1. Check for accuracy
2. Add any personal touches
3. Adjust tone if needed
4. Send

## Real Example

**Incoming email:**
> "Hi, I found your portfolio through Sarah Chen's recommendation. We're a small marketing agency looking for someone to help with website copy for our clients. We have about 3-4 projects per month. Would love to chat about working together."

**Prompt:**
```
Template: Initial Inquiry Response
Context: Referral from Sarah Chen, agency with ongoing work potential, 3-4 projects/month
```

**AI Output:**
> Hi [Name],
> 
> Thanks so much for reaching out - and please thank Sarah for the kind referral! I've enjoyed working with her team.
> 
> Website copy for marketing agencies is right in my wheelhouse. I love the variety of working across different client industries, and a consistent 3-4 projects monthly is exactly the kind of partnership I look for.
> 
> I'd love to learn more about your typical projects and how you like to work with freelancers. Are you free for a 20-minute call this week? I'm available [suggest 2-3 times].
> 
> Looking forward to it,
> [Your name]

**Your edit:** Add specific times, maybe mention a relevant portfolio piece.

## The Workflow

### For Quick Responses (2 min)
1. Read incoming email
2. Open Claude in a tab
3. Paste email + template name
4. Copy response, paste into email, quick edit, send

### For Important Emails (5 min)
1. Read incoming email carefully
2. Note specific points to address
3. Generate AI draft with detailed context
4. Edit thoroughly for tone and accuracy
5. Send

### For Batch Processing (15 min)
1. Collect emails needing responses
2. Generate all drafts in one AI session
3. Review and edit each
4. Send in batch

## What to Keep Human

- **Difficult conversations** - Scope changes, complaints, bad news
- **Negotiations** - Pricing, terms, deadlines
- **Relationship building** - Personal check-ins, congratulations
- **Sensitive topics** - Anything requiring emotional intelligence

AI handles the routine. You handle the relationships.
